# Flozi avatar generation

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, with `transparent_background: true`.
Asset: `Sources/Flozi/Resources/flozi-atlas.png` (1254 × 1254 RGBA).
The app renders the 4 × 4 atlas as sprite cells without modifying the source asset.

## Exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent 4 by 4 sprite atlas for Flozi, a cute native macOS notch companion. Create ONE square PNG with actual alpha transparency, 4 equal columns and 4 equal rows, precisely 16 square cells. In every cell place the SAME original adorable plump floating lavender baby cloud-ghost creature, soft clay-toy 3D render, pearlescent lilac body with softly scalloped bottom, two tiny arms, glossy dark eyes, soft pink cheeks, no legs, friendly and cozy. Consistent identity, front three-quarter view, camera, soft studio lighting from upper left, body scale and center in every cell. Each complete silhouette and its tiny status prop stays inside its own cell with at least 15% transparent padding on all four sides. No overlapping cells. No floor, no background, no grid lines, no text, letters, logos, badges, numbers or labels. In row-major order, exact pose expressions: row1 cell1 idle relaxed open eyes tiny smile, cell2 thinking looking up with one hand on chin and two tiny floating lilac thought spheres, cell3 working focused with arms busy and tiny mint energy spark, cell4 reading holding a little cream open book. Row2 cell1 searching holding a little mint magnifying glass, cell2 coding with a miniature dark laptop and mint keys, cell3 testing inspecting two tiny mint test cubes, cell4 waiting hands together attentive with a small amber hourglass. Row3 cell1 success gleeful closed eyes arms raised with three mint confetti shapes, cell2 error concerned eyebrows with one tiny peach exclamation-shaped abstract spark NO TEXT, cell3 interrupted gently startled with hands forward, cell4 sleeping eyes closed leaning on a small lavender pillow. Row4 cell1 offline with dim little gray cloud companion and concerned face, cell2 uncertain with head tilted and small shrug, cell3 alternate thinking frame same as row1 cell2 but eyes look sideways and one thought sphere moves, cell4 alternate idle frame same as row1 cell1 but eyes blink shut. Transparent real alpha everywhere outside the figures. Render beautiful tangible rounded 3D forms with clear highlights, subtle material shading and simple readable silhouettes, not flat vector. This is one spritesheet asset, not a presentation board.
